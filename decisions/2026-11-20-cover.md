# The daily cover, built (roadmap item 4)

Built on 2026-11-20 by a session started from another, with nobody watching: every choice below
is Claude's, taken as the recommended option and logged for Valentin to review. IDs CV1 to CV10.
It builds the approved specimen (BR35, BR39 to BR41), the grim day (BR37), the cover as the
issue's first screen (BR14) and the cover as the opened mark (BR32), with the photo found by a
search each run (V38 in x4-reader) and one composer for the phone and the X4 (V36).

## What was built

`paper cover --date D --claim C (--photo ID | --grim) [--crop l,t,r,b] [--size WxH] [--theme
light|dark] [--lang en|hu] [--out FILE]` draws the cover (`src/paper/cover.py`). Every measure is
the specimen's at 390 px wide times W / 390. With `--out` it writes one PNG at `--size` (the X4's
call); without, it writes the phone's pair into `edition/<day>/` with `cover.json`, which
`paper publish` reads into the front page. `paper cover search <words>` prints CC0 candidates
from the three museums. `skills/today` draws the cover before publishing. The phone opens on it;
one tap passes it.

Checked against the specimen with the same Cleveland record, claim and date: the photo starts at
96 px and is 500 tall at 390 × 844, as drawn in the canvas.

## CV1 · The phone's picture
**Chosen:** a JPEG at quality 90, drawn at twice 390 × 844 (780 × 1688), light and dark both
shipped. About 0.4 MB each, 0.8 MB a morning. Twice is the density the specimen was approved at
(its photo was 750 × 1000 for a 390 × 500 frame).
**Not chosen:** PNG at twice (about 1 MB each: the grain does not compress); PNG at 390 × 844 (soft
text on a 3× screen); the claim and wordmark as live text over the photo alone (crisp and about
0.15 MB, but a second layout to keep in step with the composer, against V36).
**Accepted cost:** the data repository's history grows by about 0.8 MB a morning, near 300 MB a
year. The files leave the tree after 30 days with the texts (D64); history keeps them.

## CV2 · How often the cover shows
**Chosen:** once an issue. The first open of the latest issue, on any of the reader's devices,
shows the cover; later opens go straight to the front page. The date in the top bar never leads
back to it.
**Not chosen:** every open of the app (a tap each time the reader comes back).
**Accepted cost:** a second device skips the cover once the first has shown it.

## CV3 · What counts as opened (implements BR32)
**Chosen:** the phone logs `{"ev": "cover", "id": "<day>"}` when the picture has loaded, not
when it is tapped. `paper days` counts a morning opened when its cover was shown that same day,
or, as before, when a piece of it was seen that day (issues before the cover, and a day the
picture could not load).
**Not chosen:** the tap (a cover seen and left would not count); the cover alone (the days
before it ships, and failed pictures, would count as unopened).
**Accepted cost:** an opened morning can now be a picture glanced at, with nothing read.

## CV4 · Room for a long claim
**Chosen:** the claim is set first at 30 px (scaled) and steps down to 20 px until the photo keeps
at least 45% of the height; the photo is 500 px (scaled) when the claim leaves room.
**Not chosen:** a fixed photo height (a long claim, or the X4's shorter screen, runs off the
bottom); cutting the claim.

## CV5 · The grim day's layout (BR37)
**Chosen:** the claim alone where the photo would start, as large as fits from 60 px down to
30 px (scaled), line height 1.12.
**Not chosen:** centred on the page; the claim at its usual size with an empty frame.

## CV6 · The date on the cover
**Chosen:** "Fri 20 Nov" as in the specimen; in Hungarian "nov. 20., péntek".

## CV7 · Mounts and plates
**Chosen:** the run looks at the drawn cover and crops a mount, plate edge or frame away with
`--crop`, as the specimen's photo was cropped by hand.
**Not chosen:** code that guesses where the picture ends; no crop (old photographs show their
mount).
**Accepted cost:** a few more steps in the run; a crop the run gets wrong ships.

## CV8 · Where photos come from
**Chosen:** the Met, Cleveland and the Art Institute only (V38). Each draw reads the museum's
record again and refuses a photo it does not mark CC0 or public domain, whatever the search said.
**Not chosen:** Wikimedia Commons too (BR11 lists it; its licence is the uploader's claim).

## CV9 · The lead claim and the grim call
**Chosen:** one sentence from the first news piece, in the paper's language, under 110
characters, in BR16's voice. Grim means deaths, a disaster, an attack or abuse. After three
searches with nothing fitting, the day is drawn as grim.
**Not chosen:** the closest picture anyway (a weak match on the first screen).
**Accepted cost:** some ordinary days show no photo.

## CV10 · No credit on the cover
**Chosen:** none, as in the approved drawing; CC0 asks for none. The record (title, artist,
museum, link) stays in `cover.json`, sealed with the edition.

## Decided without asking (engineering)
Pillow added as a dependency to draw and save images. IBM Plex Mono Regular and Medium are
vendored as TTF in `src/paper/fonts/` under the OFL; the serif is the reader's own Paper Serif
(Source Serif 4), unpacked from its woff2 to TTF beside them, set at weight 600 with its optical size. The grain is seeded from the date, so
the same inputs give the same bytes. The page's Content-Security-Policy now allows `blob:`
images, for the decrypted picture.

## Not covered
A real iPhone; the cover offline after the cache was cleared; the X4's grey conversion (the X4
session's); Cleveland's print-size images are several MB to download each run.
